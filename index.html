Portfolio / Project 02

Retail / Inventory & point of sale

POS System.

From warehouse to store to sales.
One system. Multiple roles.

A multi-role inventory and point-of-sale system that tracks stock across warehouse, store, and sales operations. It supports restocking, salesman stock checkout and returns, cashier checkout, returns and refunds, and merchant reporting.

A mobile app for salesmen and a Windows desktop app for cashiers, connected to the same backend and inventory data.

  • Web
  • App · Capacitor
  • Windows · Electron
My role
Full-stack developer
Scope
Team development & maintenance
Technology
ReactJS · C# · pgAdmin

01 / People & responsibilities

Five roles, one connected workflow.

Each role supports a different part of the stock and sales lifecycle, from receiving inventory to completing a sale and reviewing reports.

  1. 01

    Merchant

    Oversees the business and reviews sales and inventory reports.

  2. 02

    Warehouse & stockkeeper

    Manages warehouse restocking and stock transfers to salesmen or stores.

  3. 03

    Store

    Holds stock and supports daily store operations.

  4. 04

    Salesman

    Processes sales from their own assigned stock and returns unsold items.

  5. 05

    Cashier

    Handles checkout from store stock, customer returns, and refunds.

02 / The experience

What the system does.

01

Warehouse restocking & stock transfer

Restock the warehouse, then transfer stock to salesmen or stores.

02

Salesman stock checkout & return

Support salesman checkout from their assigned stock and track unsold items returned.

03

Cashier checkout, returns & refunds

Support checkout from store stock and handle customer returns and refunds.

04

Real-time stock tracking

Keep stock levels visible as inventory moves through the business.

05

Sales & inventory reports

Give merchants a view of sales activity and inventory for business oversight.
